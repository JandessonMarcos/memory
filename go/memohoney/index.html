What's inside MemoHoney

Each ingredient was selected for its ability to increase cerebral blood flow, deliver energy to neurons, and protect the connections that time and stress have weakened.

L-Arginine

An amino acid the body converts directly into nitric oxide — the molecule responsible for dilating blood vessels in the brain. With wider circulation pathways, more oxygen and nutrients reach your neurons, reducing those frustrating “blanks” when trying to recall a name, a word, or where you left your keys.

L-Arginine AKG 2:1 Extract

An advanced form of arginine bound to alpha-ketoglutarate, which improves absorption and extends the vasodilating effect throughout the day. It acts as a slow-release fuel for brain tissue, sustaining focus and mental sharpness without energy spikes or crashes.

L-Citrulline HCL

A precursor the body recycles back into arginine, creating a continuous cycle of nitric oxide production. This keeps cerebral blood vessels relaxed and blood flow steady even during moments of stress, fatigue, or poor sleep.

L-Citrulline Malate

Combines citrulline with malate, a compound that plays a direct role in energy production within cells. Beyond sustaining vasodilation, it helps eliminate metabolic waste that causes brain fatigue — that heavy-headed feeling at the end of the day.

Niacin (Vitamin B3)

An essential vitamin for the production of NAD+, the molecule that powers the energy machinery inside every neuron. It also improves cerebral microcirculation, ensuring the nutrients in the formula reach exactly where they need to act.

Beta-Alanine

An amino acid that raises carnosine levels in the brain, a natural antioxidant that protects neurons from age-related wear and tear. It works as a shield that preserves brain cells from deterioration caused by time, stress, and demanding daily routines.
