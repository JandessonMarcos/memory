What is the Horse Boost refund policy?

HorseBoost is sold through its official order page as a one-time purchase in packages of 2, 3 or 6 bottles. Every official order carries a 60-day money-back guarantee. In plain terms: if you try it and decide it isn't for you, you can ask for your money back within that window.

Two conditions matter more than anything else on this page.

  • It must be an official order. The guarantee belongs to the seller, not to the product name. Bottles bought on Amazon, eBay, a social media shop or any other third-party listing aren't covered.
  • It must be within 60 days. Miss the window and the promise no longer applies, however good your reason.

Everything else is process. Get the process right and the guarantee works the way it's described.

What does the HorseBoost order page actually promise?

Here's what the official order page says, in substance: a 60-day guarantee under which the company will refund every penny, no questions asked. That's a strong promise, and it's stronger than a lot of supplement guarantees, which limit refunds to unopened bottles or a single bottle.

What the order page doesn't spell out, at least not in what we've reviewed, are the mechanics. We won't guess at them, because guessing is how people lose refunds. Who qualifies for a Horse Boost refund?

Based on what the order page states, you qualify if you bought through the official checkout and you request the refund within 60 days. The order page doesn't list reasons you have to give. "No questions asked" means you don't need to prove the gummies didn't work for you.

You don't qualify, at least not through the brand, if any of these apply.

  • You bought from a marketplace or reseller. Your options are that platform's return rules. Our guide on where to buy Horse Boost explains why third-party bottles fall outside the guarantee.
  • You bought from a copycat site. If the checkout wasn't the official one, contact your card issuer instead.
  • The 60 days have passed. Plan your decision date early, as described below.

Not sure whether your order was official? Look at your confirmation email and card statement. How do you request a Horse Boost refund, step by step?

Follow these steps in order. Each one creates a record you may need later.

  • 1. Find your order details. Locate your confirmation email, order number, the date you ordered and the date the package arrived. Screenshot them.
  • 2. Check your deadline. Count 60 days from the earlier of the two dates unless support confirms otherwise. Write the deadline on your calendar.
  • 3. Contact customer support in writing. Use the contact method on your receipt or the official returns page. The HorseBoost label also prints a distributor phone number and email. Email is better than phone because it leaves a record.
  • 4. State your request plainly. Give your name, order number and order date, and say you're requesting a refund under the 60-day money-back guarantee. You don't need to explain why.
  • 5. Ask the questions from the table above. Return address, whether to send the bottles, return shipping, and how long processing takes.
  • 6. Wait for written instructions. Don't ship anything until you have them. If support gives you a reference or authorization number, write it on the package as instructed.
  • 7. Ship with tracking. If bottles need to go back, use a trackable service and keep the receipt and tracking number.
  • 8. Follow up on your timeline. If the refund hasn't appeared after the processing time support gave you, reply to the same email thread with your tracking number.
  • 9. Check your statement. Make sure the refund matches what you were told, then file all of it in one folder.

A short, polite email that hits every point in step 4 tends to get handled faster than a long one. Support teams process these in volume. Make yours easy.

Something like this works: "Hello, I'd like to request a refund under the 60-day money-back guarantee for order number [your number], placed on [date] and delivered on [date]. Please send the return instructions, including the return address and whether you need the bottles back. Thank you." Fill in the brackets, send it, and save the sent copy.

When does the 60-day refund window start?

This is the most common source of missed refunds. The order page states 60 days. Whether those days count from the order date or the delivery date is something to confirm with support, and until you have that answer, assume the earlier date.

Here's why it matters. If shipping takes a week, counting from delivery when the company counts from the order could leave you seven days short. That's an avoidable loss.

A simple plan: write your order date on the bottle cap or on your phone the day it arrives, set a reminder for day 45, and make your keep-or-refund decision by day 50. That leaves ten days for emails, instructions and shipping.

How long does a Horse Boost refund take to arrive?

Ask support for the processing time when you make your request, and write it down. Once the seller processes a refund, it typically shows up on a card statement within a few business days to a couple of weeks, depending on your bank. Debit cards and some banks are slower than credit cards.

If the refund appears as a pending credit, leave it alone. It usually posts on its own. If it never appears, reply to your support thread with your order number and any tracking number before taking further steps.

What if your refund request goes unanswered?

Most requests are handled without drama. If yours isn't, escalate in this order.

  • Follow up in the same thread. Wait the processing time support gave you, or about a week if they didn't give one, and send a short reminder.
  • Try the other contact channel. If you emailed, call, and note the date, time and name of whoever you spoke with.
  • Contact your card issuer. If you paid by credit card and the seller hasn't honored the published guarantee, you can ask your issuer about a dispute. Have your order confirmation, emails and tracking ready.
  • Report it. Unresolved problems with online sellers can be reported to the FTC.

A chargeback should be a last resort. Filing one while a refund is already being processed can slow both down.

Who should skip HorseBoost and avoid the refund process altogether?

The easiest refund is the one you never need. Talk to your doctor before ordering, and probably skip it, if you take nitrates, a prescription erectile dysfunction drug or blood pressure medication. L-arginine in the blend can lower blood pressure, and the herbs haven't been tested in combination with those drugs. Men who are very sensitive to caffeine should know each gummy has 5 mg, plus green tea extract.

And if erection problems are what sent you looking, see a doctor first. New or persistent erection problems can be an early sign of heart disease, diabetes or low testosterone. HorseBoost is a vitality supplement focused on energy and stamina. It doesn't treat erectile dysfunction, and a refund window is no substitute for a checkup. Frequently asked questions

Can I get a refund on an empty HorseBoost bottle?

The order page promises to refund every penny with no questions asked within 60 days, which suggests opened or used bottles are included. Confirm it with support in writing before you ship anything, and keep that email.

Does the HorseBoost guarantee cover all six bottles?

The guarantee is described for official orders within 60 days. How unopened and opened bottles in a larger package are handled is a detail to confirm on the official returns page before you send them back.

Do I get my shipping cost back?

The order page doesn't spell this out in what we've reviewed. The 2-bottle package includes a shipping charge, so ask support whether original and return shipping are refunded when you make your request.

Can I return Horse Boost I bought on Amazon to the official seller?

No. Only orders placed through the official checkout carry the 60-day guarantee. Use the marketplace's return process for anything bought there.

Do I need to explain why I want a refund?

No. The order page says no questions asked. A short email with your name, order number, order date and a clear refund request under the 60-day guarantee is enough.

Will I be charged again after a refund?

The official order page describes a one-time charge with no subscription. If you ever see a charge you didn't expect, contact support with your order number and check with your card issuer.

Can I reorder after getting a refund?

Nothing on the order page says otherwise, but there's little reason to. If HorseBoost didn't help over a fair trial, a second round is unlikely to change that.
